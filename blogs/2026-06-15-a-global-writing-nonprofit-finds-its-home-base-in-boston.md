Write the World Inc. is a global writing nonprofit serving teen writers, parents, and educators in more than 190 countries. At Industrious Harvard Square, the mostly virtual organization has found something essential: a physical home base for collaboration, local partnerships, and the kind of in-person conversations that help its mission grow.
